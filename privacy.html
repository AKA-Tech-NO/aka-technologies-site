Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026

Who we are

AKA Bounce is provided by AKA Technologies AS. Questions about privacy can be sent to raymond@aka-technologies.no.

Game data

Your last score and best score are stored locally on your device. AKA Technologies does not require an account and does not receive this score data.

Advertising

AKA Bounce uses Google AdMob to show non-personalized advertisements between completed game rounds. Ads are not shown during active gameplay. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, ad interactions and approximate location derived from the IP address to deliver, secure and measure advertising.

Consent and choices

Where required, Google’s consent platform asks for your choices before advertising is requested. If consent or eligibility is unavailable, the app is designed not to request advertising. You can also reset or limit advertising identifiers in your device settings.

Children

AKA Bounce is not directed to children. Advertising is disabled when adult eligibility cannot be established.

Third parties and retention

Google processes advertising data under its own policies and retention rules. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to data controlled by AKA Technologies AS.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
